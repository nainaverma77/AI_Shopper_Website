📦 Orders & Shipping

How do I track my order? ▼
Go to My Account → My Orders. You'll see the current status of each order. For detailed tracking, click on the order number. You'll receive email and SMS updates at each stage of delivery.
How long does delivery take? ▼
Standard delivery takes 3-7 business days depending on your location. Metro cities typically receive orders in 3-4 days. Express delivery (available on select products) delivers within 1-2 business days.
Is Cash on Delivery (COD) available? ▼
Yes! COD is available on most products. You can select "Cash on Delivery" as your payment method during checkout. COD orders have a maximum limit of ₹50,000.
Can I change my delivery address? ▼
You can change your delivery address before the order is shipped. Go to My Account → My Orders → click on the order, and use the "Change Address" option. Once shipped, the address cannot be modified.

🔄 Returns & Exchange

What is the return policy? ▼
We offer a 7-day easy return policy on most products. Items must be unused, in original packaging, with all tags intact. Electronics have a 10-day replacement window. Fashion items can be exchanged for different sizes.
How do I return a product? ▼
Go to My Account → My Orders → Select the order → Click "Return/Exchange". Choose your reason, and a pickup will be scheduled within 24-48 hours. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the item.
When will I get my refund? ▼
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after the returned item is received. Bank transfers take 3-5 days. UPI refunds are instant. For COD orders, refunds are credited to your AiShopper wallet.
Can I exchange for a different product? ▼
Exchange is available for size/color changes within the same product. For a completely different product, please return the current item and place a new order. Our customer support can help facilitate this process.

💳 Payments

What payment methods are accepted? ▼
We accept Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking (all major banks), and Cash on Delivery. We also support AiShopper Wallet for instant payments.
Is online payment safe? ▼
Absolutely! All transactions are encrypted with 256-bit SSL. We use Razorpay/Stripe for payment processing — the same technology used by top banks. We never store your card details on our servers.
How do I apply a coupon code? ▼
In the cart page, you'll find a "Coupon Code" input field below the order summary. Enter your coupon code and click "Apply". Valid coupons will be reflected in the total immediately. Some coupons have minimum order value requirements.

👤 Account

How do I create an account? ▼
Click on the user icon in the top right → "Login / Sign Up". Switch to the Sign Up tab, enter your name, email, and password (minimum 6 characters), and you're done! You can also continue as a guest without an account.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it? ▼
On the login page, click "Forgot Password?" and enter your registered email. You'll receive a password reset link within a few minutes. If you don't see the email, check your spam folder or contact support.
How do I delete my account? ▼
Go to My Account → Settings → "Delete Account" in the Danger Zone. This action is permanent and will delete all your order history, saved addresses, and preferences. Active orders will still be fulfilled.
